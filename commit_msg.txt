💡 What: Added aria-hidden="true" to four FontAwesome <i> tags in aula-2.md.
🎯 Why: Prevents screen readers from announcing decorative icons and their textual fallbacks (like ":map-marked-alt:"), reducing noise for users relying on assistive technology.

♿ Accessibility: Improved screen reader experience by hiding decorative elements.
