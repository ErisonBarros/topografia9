🎨 Palette: Add aria-hidden to decorative icons in aula-2.md

💡 What:
Added `aria-hidden="true"` to raw HTML decorative FontAwesome `<i>` tags embedded in `guitbook/aulas/aula-2.md` and created Palette's journal in `.Jules/palette.md`.

🎯 Why:
To prevent screen readers from uselessly reading out the textual fallbacks (e.g., ":map-marked-alt:") of purely decorative icons, providing a smoother experience for users with visual impairments.

📸 Before/After:
N/A (Invisible accessibility change)

♿ Accessibility:
Ensures decorative icons are properly hidden from assistive technologies.
