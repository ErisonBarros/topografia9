🎨 Palette: Add aria-hidden to decorative FontAwesome icons

💡 What: Added `aria-hidden="true"` to raw HTML decorative FontAwesome `<i>` tags embedded in `aula-2.md` and added a journal entry in `.Jules/palette.md`.
🎯 Why: Without this attribute, screen readers read out the textual fallbacks of these icons (like `:map-marked-alt:`), which degrades the listening experience for visually impaired users.
📸 Before/After: N/A (non-visual change)
♿ Accessibility: Improved screen reader experience by hiding decorative icons that provide no additional semantic value.
