🎨 Palette: Hide decorative FontAwesome icons from screen readers

💡 What: Added aria-hidden="true" to raw HTML FontAwesome <i> tags embedded in GitBook markdown.
🎯 Why: Prevents screen readers from unnecessarily reading textual fallbacks inside purely decorative icons (e.g., :map-marked-alt:).
📸 Before/After: Visual presentation unchanged, purely an accessibility structural fix.
♿ Accessibility: Improved screen reader experience by reducing noise from decorative elements.
